## 🚀 Start in 30 seconds

1. Open the link above in **Chrome** (or Safari on iPhone).
2. Tap **Load a sample…** and pick *Busy work day*, *Study day* or *Slow weekend* to see how it looks.
3. Tap **Clear day** when you're ready for your own plan. There's an **Undo** if you tap it by mistake.
4. Start typing into any hour. Tick it when it's done.

## 🧭 Pick your path

<details>
<summary><b>📅 I want to plan my day</b></summary>

- **Schedule:** one line per hour, from 6 am to 12 am. The current hour glows.
- **Three things that matter today:** your top priorities, kept simple.
- **To do:** tag each task *Low*, *Med* or *High*. Tap the arrow to send a task to tomorrow.
- **Bring over unfinished from yesterday:** one tap, nothing gets lost.
- **Focus timer:** 15, 25 or 50 minutes. It beeps when you finish and logs your focus time.
- **Water, mood, notes, intention and evening review:** the small habits that make a day feel complete.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🔁 I want routines (gym, study, anything)</b></summary>

A routine is a main task that repeats by itself and holds its own checklist. **You decide what it is.**

- Name it anything: *Gym*, *Learn Python*, *Guitar*, *Prayer*.
- Choose the days it repeats (M T W T F S S).
- Set a start date, and an optional end date. No end date means it repeats forever.
- Add sub-tasks, and give each sub-task its own days. Push day on Monday, legs on Tuesday, chapter review on Friday.
- Add a **note for today** (weights lifted, topic covered).
- Watch your **streak** grow.
- Not sure where to start? Use a template: *Workout plan*, *Study block* or *Morning routine*, then change anything.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📈 I want to see my progress</b></summary>

- Switch between **7, 14 and 30 days**.
- Four summary tiles: average, best day, items finished, days at 80% or more.
- Hover over a bar (or tap it) to open that day.
- Tap **Customise** to choose **Bars, Line, Area or Dots**, pick a colour, and show or hide values and the average line.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🎨 I want it to look like me</b></summary>

Open **Theme** at the top.

- **Background:** presets, or upload your own photo or short video from your computer or phone.
- **Readability veil:** fade the background so text stays easy to read.
- **Smart text:** with *Automatic* colour, the text turns dark or light to match your background.
- **Fonts:** Default, Handwritten, Serif, Rounded, Typewriter.
- **Text colour:** pick from the swatches or choose any custom colour.
- **Graph background:** give the progress graph its own image or video, with its own opacity.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📱 I'm on my phone</b></summary>

1. Open the link in **Chrome**. Don't open the file in Docs or Drive, because they can't run it.
2. Tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen**.
3. Use the **Prev / Today / Next** bar at the bottom to move between days.

Your plans live on each device, so the phone starts empty. To move your data over, open **Backup and restore** at the bottom of the page, copy the text, then paste it on the other device and press **Restore**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🔒 What happens to my data?</b></summary>

Everything stays in **your own browser**, one page per date. There's no account, no server and nobody else can see it. Clearing your browser data erases your plans, so use **Backup and restore** now and then.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🕉️ What's the quote at the top?</b></summary>

A short line from the Bhagavad Gita, different each day. The same date always shows the same verse.

</details>

## 🛠️ Good to know
